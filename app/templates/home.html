Understanding the Thyroid Engine

The thyroid is a butterfly-shaped gland at the base of your neck. It acts as the master control engine of your metabolism — regulating energy use, body temperature, heart rate, and weight through the secretion of highly specialized hormones.

TSH: Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (from brain) T4: Thyroxine (Main hormone) T3: Triiodothyronine (Active hormone)

Clinical Myths vs Facts

Myth Thyroid problems only affect older women.
Fact Can affect men, infants, teens, and individuals of all ages.
Myth A lump in the neck always means cancer.
Fact Over 95% of thyroid nodules detected are benign (non-cancerous).
Myth Extra iodine supplementation always helps.
Fact Too much iodine can actually trigger or worsen Hashimoto’s disease.

Key Pathologies

Hypothyroidism

Underactive / Insufficient hormone production

  • Unexplained weight gain
  • Chronic fatigue & lethargy
  • Cold intolerance

Hyperthyroidism

Overactive / Excess hormone production

  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Clinical anxiety & tremors
  • Heat intolerance & sweating

Proactive Prevention Tips

  • Dietary Intake: Ensure adequate iodine (iodized salt, seafood, dairy) without over-supplementing.
  • Endocrine Disruptors: Minimize exposure to BPAs and certain plastics that can interfere with hormone signaling.
  • Routine Screening: Request an annual TSH blood test during physicals, especially if family history exists.
